# 2008 Source Access Note

The source of record for this year remains the Ministry of Justice pages listed in `README.md`.

During machine-readable extraction, the current web fetch path returned HTTP 403 for direct Ministry of Justice page/PDF opening. Two secondary access paths were therefore used:

1. Kagoshima University Judicial Policy Education and Research Center's archive of Ministry of Justice / Judicial Examination Committee documents, for the official 2008 grading commentary and examiner-hearing text.
2. A public transcription page was consulted only to recover / cross-check the machine-readable wording of the 2008 problem and official question-intent text that could not be directly opened through the Ministry of Justice endpoint in this session.

The public transcription was **not** treated as legal authority or as a model answer. The substantive year extraction is anchored to the official examiner signals and official source-of-record pages. Nevertheless, this access path is recorded because SKALE treats provenance honestly rather than pretending a source was not exposed.

If strict official-binary reproducibility is required before cross-year synthesis, archive the original Ministry of Justice PDFs (with retrieval date and checksum) and re-run the year extraction against those binaries before promoting the 2008 observation into the canonical model.
