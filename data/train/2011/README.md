## Problem capsule

A company provides an Internet map service containing panoramic street-view images. The images can reveal faces, houses, gardens, balconies, interiors, and patterns of private life. A statute regulates the service to protect individuals and allows the responsible minister to recommend corrective measures and ultimately order cessation. X company masks directly identifying information and lowers its camera height but refuses to correct images revealing private life where the statute does not expressly list the image type. X receives a cessation order.

The examinee must identify the litigation route and construct X's constitutional claims, then give an independent view while anticipating the government's response.
